About BACKED

Empowering local communities through innovative investing solutions, BACKED connects small businesses with local investors to create sustainable growth and shared prosperity.

Our Mission

BACKED was founded on a simple but powerful idea: what if people could directly invest in the small businesses they love in their communities?

Traditional financing options often leave small businesses behind, while everyday investors have limited access to meaningful local investment opportunities. BACKED bridges this gap, creating a platform where community members can invest in local businesses they believe in, while helping those businesses thrive.

By partnering with Capital One, we're able to provide a secure, transparent, and accessible platform that makes community investing simple for everyone.
